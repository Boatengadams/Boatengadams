## 👨🏽‍💻 About Me

I’m **Adams Boateng**, a Computer Science student at **Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology (KNUST)**. I build software across the stack, from the interface and backend services to the cloud infrastructure and security model underneath them.

My work is guided by a simple standard: **build it, understand it, secure it, and ship it**. I’m especially interested in practical systems that solve real business problems and remain maintainable after the first release.

### What I Do

* Build full-stack web applications and business platforms
* Design responsive frontend experiences with React, React Native, and Expo
* Develop backend services, APIs, authentication, and database systems
* Deploy applications with cloud infrastructure and CI/CD workflows
* Explore application security, penetration testing, and secure design
* Experiment with AI, computer vision, and intelligent automation
* Model real-world workflows involving payments, inventory, users, and roles

I’m currently pursuing opportunities in **Software Engineering**, **Full-Stack Development**, **Frontend Development**, and **Backend Development**, with particular interest in cloud, application security, and AI-enabled products.

---

## 🧠 Engineering Philosophy

> **Build it. Understand it. Secure it. Ship it.**

Good software is clear enough to understand, resilient enough to trust, and simple enough to extend. I care about the parts that rarely appear in a portfolio screenshot: data boundaries, authorization, failure handling, maintainability, and the path from local development to production.

I build with these principles in mind:

* **Maintainable** — structured for future development
* **Secure** — designed with authentication, authorization, validation, and data protection
* **Scalable** — able to grow with users and business requirements
* **Usable** — designed around real users and real workflows
* **Deployable** — built with production environments in mind

# 🚀 Selected Projects

### 🎓 EduTec — School Management & Digital Learning Platform

**Protecting data. Creating futures.**

A multi-role school management platform designed to bring administration, teaching, student records, attendance, assessments, results, and parent communication into one digital workflow.

**What it demonstrates:** role-based access, school-isolated data, attendance and results workflows, parent communication, Firebase services, and security-focused access control.

`Full-Stack Development` · `Education Technology` · `Firebase` · `Security`

---


### ⚡ Cofkans Electricals — Multi-Branch Business Platform

A production-oriented digital platform designed for a multi-branch electrical products business.

The system combines a public ecommerce experience with internal operational tools for managing products, branches, staff, inventory, orders, payments, and business workflows.

**Key areas:**

* Multi-branch inventory management
* Product and stock management
* Staff role management
* Order and fulfillment workflows
* Stock transfers between branches
* Payment integration
* Audit logging
* Role-based authorization
* Firebase backend infrastructure
* Cloud functions and server-side workflows
* Cloudflare security infrastructure

**Focus:** Full-Stack Development · Business Systems · Cloud · Security · Payments

---

### 🔑 BAGS-KEY SYSTEM — University Accommodation Platform

A secure accommodation and room-key management concept designed for university environments.

The platform is intended to connect students with their assigned:

**Institution → Hall/Hostel → Room → Roommates → Accommodation Information**

The architecture emphasizes institution-isolated access, role-based permissions, and secure accommodation data management.

**Focus:** React Native · Expo · Authentication · Secure Data Architecture · University Technology

---

### 🌌 Solar System Simulator

An educational C++ simulation focused on helping students understand fundamental astronomy and physics concepts through interactive visualization.

The project explores:

* Gravity
* Velocity
* Acceleration
* Orbital motion
* Centripetal force
* Planetary classification
* Earth–Moon systems
* Gravitational direction and magnitude
* Interactive educational overlays

**Focus:** Modern C++ · Raylib · Physics Simulation · Educational Technology

### 👁️ AI Suspicious Activity & Object Detection
